# For Registered Users

## Logging In

Tap the person icon in the top-right corner of the app bar to open the
login dialog. Enter your username and password and press **Login**.

![Login Dialog](/manual/images/login-dialog.png 'Login Dialog')

## Photos & Uploads

### Uploading from the Gallery

When logged in, a **camera upload button** appears at the bottom-right of
the photo gallery page.

![Upload Button](/manual/images/image-upload-button.png 'Upload Button')

Tap it to select a photo from your device and upload it to the event gallery.

### Managing Your Uploads

Open the **Uploads** section from the navigation menu (available when logged
in) to:

- See all photos you have uploaded
- Preview a photo by tapping its thumbnail
- Delete a photo you uploaded

![Upload Management](/manual/images/upload-table.png 'Upload Management')

::: admonition tip
Your uploads appear in the public gallery immediately after uploading. If you
accidentally upload the wrong photo, you can remove it from the Uploads page.
:::
